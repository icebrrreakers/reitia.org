REITIA II — 4 July 2026

A participatory ritual-performance on the banks of the Brenta River, combining clay, fire, water, landscape and cultural memory.

Participants will shape small clay talismans by the river, fire them in a Paleovenetan pit kiln with master ceramist Antonio Bonaldi, and cross a symbolic threshold through embodied practices and immersion in the Brenta landscape.

The day will continue with lunch at Ca’ Rezzonico Alfredina.

About

REITIA is a transdisciplinary platform exploring embodied heritage, participatory performance and place-responsive cultural practices across contemporary Europe.

Through public events, ritual performance, environmental immersion and artistic collaborations, REITIA investigates how landscapes, cultural memory and local narratives can become active agents in collective transformation.

Chi siamo

REITIA è una piattaforma transdisciplinare che esplora patrimonio incarnato, performance partecipativa e pratiche culturali site-responsive nell’Europa contemporanea.

Attraverso eventi pubblici, pratiche rituali, immersione ambientale e collaborazioni artistiche, REITIA indaga come paesaggi, memoria culturale e narrazioni territoriali possano diventare agenti attivi di trasformazione collettiva.
